Training pipeline

Numeric property features and locality categories are transformed, then estimated by a HistGradientBoostingRegressor. Navi Mumbai and Mumbai/BMC use separate models so one market does not distort the other.

How to read an estimate

Use the price as a transparent market guide, not a final valuation. Building condition, floor, exact micro-location, parking, title status and negotiation can move an actual sale price.
